# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenQuiz is a community project. We want participation to be welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Welcome different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy and keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove content or restrict participation when needed. To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub's repository contact or security reporting feature.
